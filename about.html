What is The Student Hub

The student hub is a all-in-one, comprehensive webpage that is meant to help students during their stage of life, providing ample of support and tools to make life easier.

Why This Website Exists

The student hub is here to help students with the following problem:

  • Anxious on Time Management
  • Existing tools are not ideal
  • Looking for Self Enrichment

What You Can Do Here

Study

  • Track your assignments
  • Calculate GPA
  • Use flash cards
  • Obtain useful resources

Life

  • Manage time
  • Plan daily schedules
  • Track personal expenses
  • Use productivity timer

Who It's For

  • Great for CS students
  • College or university students
  • Anyone who prefers simple tools
