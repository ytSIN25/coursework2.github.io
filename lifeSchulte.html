Schulte Grid
A Mini Game to Train Your Focusing Skill.

What is Schulte Grid

Schulte Grid, also known as Schulte Table is a grid filled with randomly placed numbers. It is originally created by German psychiatrist Walter Schulte to assess attention disorders, but it can also be played as a traning for longer attention span and faster reading speed.

How to Play

Starting from 1, click one-by-one as fast as possible until 25 and the timer will record your time taken. The clicked numbers will not disappear so you must also remember what number is next.
