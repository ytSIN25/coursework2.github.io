What is Pomodoro Timer

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. This technique uses a kitchen timer to break work into intervals which is known as a pomodoro, from the Italian word for tomato, after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer that Cirillo used while he was a university student.

How to Use

Set up your desired time on top, and start focusing your job or homework. After 25 minute, you can take a short break as the timer shows, after two sets of work and break you can have a 30 minute of long rest. During the focus 25 minute, do your best to deter any distraction and just focus on the thing you want to do to maximize your efficiency.
